# IOFI Job & Task Library – Engineering, Administration, Marine, Construction & Warehousing

**Document ID:** IOFI-JL-11-15 | **Version:** 0.1 | **Date:** 14 November 2026  
**Status:** Working draft for field and professional review  
**Scope:** Petroleum and natural-gas operating companies, production and processing sites, project sites, warehouses, marine terminals and offshore installations where applicable.

> This document is a task library draft, not a validated exposure assessment, clinical standard, legal determination, risk score or fitness decision. Verify applicability from actual work, site conditions, process and worker assignment. Job title alone does not establish exposure.

## 1. Common IOFI evidence rules

Every task profile should distinguish: (a) health exposure evidence for OERI; (b) operational safety hazards for TRA / Critical Safety Gate; (c) essential physical, sensory, cognitive and psychosocial demands for JTCI; (d) physician-only health relevance for IHVI; and (e) worksite context for WORM.

Record source, date, measurement method, unit, averaging period, task frequency/duration, control verification and confidence. Use **Unknown / Pending verification** when evidence is missing. Do not enter missing evidence as zero. Do not invent numerical cut-offs, scoring weights or job fitness rules.

Health hazards are conditional and task-specific. For example, welding fumes require actual welding or nearby exposure; silica requires relevant material/task evidence; marine work does not automatically imply chemical exposure; construction tasks do not all involve work at height; warehouse staff are not presumed to handle every stored chemical.

## 2. Family 11 – Engineering & Technical Support

### Job profiles

| Job ID | Position | Typical scope |
|---|---|---|
| ENG-PROC | Process Engineer | Process monitoring, troubleshooting, process-safety reviews and field visits |
| ENG-MECH | Mechanical Engineer | Rotating/static equipment review, inspections, maintenance planning |
| ENG-ELEC | Electrical Engineer | Electrical systems, inspections, testing and engineering modifications |
| ENG-INST | Instrumentation & Control Engineer | Instrument systems, control loops, calibration oversight and field troubleshooting |
| ENG-CIVIL | Civil / Structural Engineer | Structural inspections, foundations, buildings, access and project support |
| ENG-PLN | Planning / Reliability Engineer | Reliability, work planning, data analysis and coordination |
| ENG-DES | Design / Project Engineer | Design reviews, drawings, contractor interfaces and site verification |
| ENG-TECH | Technical Specialist / Field Support | Specialist diagnostics, measurements and troubleshooting |

### Task catalogue

| Task ID | Task | Potential health exposures | Operational safety hazards / demands |
|---|---|---|---|
| ENG-T01 | Office-based engineering design and calculations | Screen/visual demand, sedentary posture, psychosocial workload | Sustained concentration, accuracy, communication; workstation ergonomics |
| ENG-T02 | Process-unit field walkdown | Noise, heat, solar exposure, process vapours only where measured/observed | Moving vehicles, process lines, slips/trips, restricted zones; situational awareness |
| ENG-T03 | Equipment inspection / vibration data collection | Noise, hand-arm or whole-body vibration depending on tools/platform, heat | Rotating machinery, stored energy, line-of-fire; safe access and isolation |
| ENG-T04 | Electrical inspection / testing | No presumed chemical exposure; task-specific contact with cleaning agents or battery gases if applicable | Electric shock, arc flash, stored electrical energy; authorization and isolation critical |
| ENG-T05 | Instrument calibration / field troubleshooting | Chemical contact only for actual calibration media/cleaners; noise/heat based on location | Pressurized impulse lines, electrical energy, process release; permit/isolation |
| ENG-T06 | Civil/structural inspection | Outdoor heat/UV, dust depending on site/material, noise near construction | Falls, dropped objects, unstable structures, traffic and excavation hazards |
| ENG-T07 | Contractor technical supervision | Heat/noise/dust depending on worksite; psychosocial load | Interface risks, simultaneous operations, access control and stop-work authority |
| ENG-T08 | Reliability data review / control-room support | Sedentary posture, screen demand, shift-related fatigue if rostered | Alarm/operational decision demands only where role has control authority |
| ENG-T09 | Site acceptance / commissioning checks | Noise, heat, process-specific chemical exposure only if task evidence supports it | Energization, pressure testing, startup, simultaneous operations and incomplete safeguards |

### Essential demands and controls

- Match field attendance and access requirements to actual site and process conditions.
- Verify competence, authorization, permits, isolation and safe systems of work for electrical, mechanical, instrumentation and pressure-related tasks.
- Use remote diagnostics and engineering controls where they reduce unnecessary exposure without compromising inspection quality.
- Capture noise, heat, dust, vapour or vibration measurements only where the task and sampling strategy justify them.
- Separate design/office tasks from field execution and emergency-response duties; do not assume all engineers have the same demands.
- Assess cognitive demand, fatigue and decision authority for roles involved in control-room support, commissioning and safety-critical technical decisions.

| Task ID | Task | Potential health exposures | Operational safety hazards / demands |
|---|---|---|---|

### Essential demands and controls

- Define office-only versus site-visiting duties clearly.
- Provide ergonomic workstation assessment, task variation and appropriate breaks.
- Manage workload, role clarity, working hours, psychosocial hazards and respectful workplace practices.
- Apply information security and confidentiality controls; clinical data must not be available merely because a manager is senior.
- Site visits require local induction and controls proportionate to the area entered.
- Do not create unnecessary medical screening or physical tests for office roles without a documented job-related basis.

### Essential demands and controls

- Verify role-specific maritime certificates, competence and applicable medical certification requirements with the relevant authority/employer.
- Assess actual vessel/platform motion, shift patterns, noise, heat, ventilation and engine-room conditions.
- Maintain permit-to-work, lifting plans, dropped-object prevention, mooring exclusion zones and man-overboard controls.
- Record actual transfer method, sea state limits, access arrangements and emergency evacuation capability.
- Include motion sickness only as a task-related functional consideration where it affects safe performance; avoid diagnosis-based assumptions.
- WORM must capture actual medevac route, weather limitations, vessel/aircraft availability, communications and receiving medical capability.

### Task catalogue

| Task ID | Task | Potential health exposures | Operational safety hazards / demands |
|---|---|---|---|
| CON-T01 | Excavation / earthworks | Dust (silica only where material/task supports it), diesel exhaust, noise, vibration | Cave-in, buried services, plant interaction, falling into excavation |
| CON-T02 | Concrete mixing / pouring | Cement dust, wet cement skin/eye contact, noise, manual handling | Plant movement, formwork failure, slips and chemical burns |
| CON-T03 | Welding / thermal cutting | Welding fumes, gases, UV/IR radiation, noise, heat | Fire/explosion, burns, electrical shock, confined-space or hot-work hazards |
| CON-T04 | Steel erection / bolting | Noise, vibration, ergonomic load, outdoor heat/cold | Falls, dropped objects, suspended loads, structural instability |
| CON-T05 | Scaffold erection/dismantling | Manual handling, dust, heat/cold | Falls, collapse, falling objects, electrical proximity |
| CON-T06 | Surface preparation / blasting | Dust and abrasive particulate, noise, vibration, coating residues | High-pressure equipment, flying particles, confined-space entry if applicable |
| CON-T07 | Painting / coating application | Solvent vapours, aerosols, skin contact, sensitizers depending on product/SDS | Fire/explosion, work at height, ventilation failure, confined-space hazards |
| CON-T08 | Heavy equipment operation | Whole-body vibration, noise, diesel exhaust, heat | Rollover, collision, blind spots, overhead/underground services |
| CON-T09 | Electrical installation / testing | Product-specific contact only where used; no generic chemical assumption | Electric shock, arc flash, temporary power, stored energy |
| CON-T10 | QA/QC inspection / NDT | Radiation only for radiographic NDT; chemical agents only for actual penetrant/cleaner use; ergonomic/screen demand | Radiation exclusion zones, pressure tests, access hazards and dropped objects |

### Essential demands and controls

- Use task-specific method statements/JSA, permit controls, competent supervision and simultaneous-operations coordination.
- Apply hierarchy of controls for dust, fumes, solvents and noise; verify local exhaust/ventilation and exposure monitoring where warranted.
- Confirm SDS and product-specific hazards for coatings, solvents, cement additives and cleaning agents.
- Control work at height, scaffold inspection/tagging, dropped objects, excavation protection and lifting operations as separate operational hazards.
- Use respiratory protection only within a managed programme with selection, fit testing, maintenance, training and medical evaluation as applicable.
- For radiographic NDT, verify authorization, radiation protection plan, controlled area, monitoring and dose records under applicable requirements.
- Do not presume every construction worker performs hot work, works at height or enters confined spaces; map each task separately.

### Essential demands and controls

- Verify racking capacity, inspection status, load stability, aisle clearance and pedestrian/vehicle segregation.
- Maintain inventory location, product identification, SDS availability, segregation compatibility and spill arrangements.
- Use mechanical aids, adjustable picking heights and task rotation based on ergonomic assessment.
- Ensure forklift authorization, inspection, seat belts/restraints where fitted, speed controls and suitable ventilation.
- Define safe cylinder handling, securing, segregation, caps/valve protection and compatible storage.
- Capture temperature, ventilation and monitoring evidence for hazardous-material stores according to actual stored products and applicable requirements.
- Do not assume a warehouse is a chemical exposure area merely because hazardous products are stored in sealed, controlled packaging.

## 7. WORM site context fields

For all five families, collect actual site type, access and travel conditions, communications, medical support, emergency response capability, evacuation route/time, environmental conditions, shift pattern, contractor interface, staffing and evidence date. Do not infer emergency response times or medical capability from site name or geographic remoteness.

## 8. Governance and validation checklist

- Verify job/task descriptions with department owners, workers and contractor representatives.
- Conduct field observations and review JSA/JHA, process diagrams, SDS, monitoring reports, maintenance logs and emergency plans.
- Have competent occupational hygienists review exposure-agent selection and measurement strategy.
- Have occupational physicians review confidential medical relevance and task-linked functional considerations.
- Keep HSE-facing output limited to restrictions and controls needed to manage the task; protect diagnoses and clinical notes.
- Confirm Egyptian legal requirements and current ministerial decisions with competent local reviewers. OSHA/NIOSH and international industry sources are technical references, not automatically Egyptian legal requirements.
- Maintain revision history, source/version, approval state and review date.
- No weights, cut-offs, automatic fitness outcomes or diagnosis-based disqualifications are authorized by this draft.
